Hellbuddy

A little hellbender that lives on top of your Mac's windows. He walks along them, rides along when you move them, naps, and loves a boop.

Download for Mac
Free · macOS 13 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

Hellbuddy is free, with a suggested donation of $2 if he makes you smile: chip in on Ko-fi.

Install

  1. Open the download. Double-click Hellbender.dmg in your Downloads, then drag Hellbender onto the Applications folder in the window that opens.
  2. Open it. Double-click Hellbender in your Applications folder. The first time, your Mac checks that it's OK to open something downloaded from the internet: click Open.
  3. Say hi. He drops onto your screen.

What he does

  • Walks along the tops of your windows, and rides along when you drag one. Yank it fast and he goes flying.
  • Click him for hearts. Drag him around and toss him (gently, he remembers).
  • Hold your cursor still near him and he'll come say hello. Swipe past fast and he'll startle.
  • Leave him be and he'll yawn, stretch, and nap.

For more, right-click him (or click the lizard in the menu bar): drop him from the sky, change his size, or quit.

Quit or remove him

To quit: right-click him (or click the lizard in the menu bar) and choose Quit. Open Hellbender from Applications to bring him back.

To remove: quit him first, then drag Hellbender from Applications to the Trash.
