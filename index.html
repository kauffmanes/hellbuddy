How it works

Your hellbuddy wanders along the tops of your windows while you work.

Lives on your windows

Walks along them, rides along when you drag one, and tumbles off when you yank it away.

Has a life of its own

Naps when it's bored, watches your cursor, and loves a boop. Drag it anywhere you like.

Gets snacks

Pick a food from the menu. It drops from the sky and your hellbuddy goes and gets it.

Install

On a Mac

  1. Open the download. Double-click Hellbuddies.dmg in your Downloads, then drag Hellbuddies onto the Applications folder in the window that opens.
  2. Open it. Double-click Hellbuddies in your Applications folder. The first time, your Mac checks that it's OK to open something downloaded from the internet: click Open.
  3. Say hi. Your hellbuddy drops onto your screen. It updates itself from then on.

On Windows

  1. Run the installer. Open Hellbuddies-Setup.exe from your Downloads.
  2. Get past the blue screen. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. You only see this once.
  3. Say hi. Your hellbuddy drops onto your screen, and keeps itself up to date from then on.

Why the blue screen? Windows shows it for apps from makers who haven't bought a code-signing certificate, which costs hundreds of dollars a year. Hellbuddies doesn't ask for any permissions and can't see what's in your windows, only where they are.

Quit or remove

To quit on a Mac: right-click your hellbuddy (or click the animal in the menu bar) and choose Quit. Open Hellbuddies from Applications to bring it back.

To remove: quit first, then drag Hellbuddies from Applications to the Trash.

To quit on Windows: right-click your hellbuddy (or its icon in the taskbar tray, by the clock) and choose Quit. Open Hellbuddies from the Start menu to bring it back.

To remove: go to Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find Hellbuddies, and choose Uninstall.

Privacy

Hellbuddies doesn't collect anything about you. It sees where your windows are, not what's in them, and checks GitHub about once a day for a newer version. When you unlock a pack, it checks your license key with Gumroad. This website counts visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you. Packs are sold through Gumroad, under its privacy policy.
