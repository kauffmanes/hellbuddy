Hellbuddy

Little hellbuddies that live on top of your windows. They walk along them, ride along when you move them, nap, and love a boop.

Download for Mac

Meet the hellbuddies

Hellbuddy the Hellbender walks along your windows, rides along when you drag them, naps, and loves a boop.

Zaduck the Mountain Goat hops along, leaps from window to window, and sometimes charges headfirst into the edge of your screen. He's a yeller.

Pick your hellbuddy any time: right-click it and choose Animal. Every hellbuddy is hand-drawn in Procreate, and more are coming soon.

Install

  1. Open the download. Double-click Hellbender.dmg in your Downloads, then drag Hellbender onto the Applications folder in the window that opens.
  2. Open it. Double-click Hellbender in your Applications folder. The first time, your Mac checks that it's OK to open something downloaded from the internet: click Open.
  3. Say hi. Your hellbuddy drops onto your screen.

Quit or remove

To quit: right-click your hellbuddy (or click the animal in the menu bar) and choose Quit. Open Hellbender from Applications to bring it back.

To remove: quit first, then drag Hellbender from Applications to the Trash.
